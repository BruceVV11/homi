Account deletion

Delete your Homi account and associated Homi data.

You can delete your account directly inside Homi. If you cannot access the app, you can also submit an account-deletion request outside the app through Concept Lab.

App: Homi Developer: Concept Lab Last updated: 29 September 2026

Option 1 · Delete directly in Homi

If you can sign in to Homi, this is the fastest path:

  1. Open Homi.
  2. Open Homi & account.
  3. Scroll to Your data.
  4. Tap Delete Homi account.
  5. Read the deletion notice and continue.
  6. Complete the recent sign-in or password confirmation if requested.
  7. Confirm Delete permanently.

Homi will remove the Homi cloud account data managed by the service and clear Homi household/location data from that device as part of the completed in-app deletion flow.

Option 2 · Request deletion outside the app

If you cannot access Homi, use Concept Lab's public contact form to initiate deletion.

  1. Open the Concept Lab contact page using the button below.
  2. State clearly that the request is a Homi account deletion request.
  3. Provide the email address used for the Homi account.
  4. State that you want the Homi account and associated Homi data deleted.
  5. Concept Lab may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account before processing the deletion.
Never send your password, Google password, authentication code, bank details or card details. They are not needed for an account-deletion request.

What deletion covers

After identity verification and a valid deletion request, Homi deletes or de-identifies account-associated information under its control, including the Homi account/profile identity and Homi connection code, trusted-connection state, location-sharing grants and latest shared location state, registered notification devices, account-owned service metadata and other Homi cloud data tied to that account.

Shared records that belong to other remaining users can be detached from the deleted account where necessary so those users do not lose legitimate Household history. For example, a record may show a neutral label such as Former Homi user instead of retaining the deleted account identity.

Limited de-identified security or billing-integrity records may remain where needed to prevent replay, maintain transaction integrity or meet legal obligations. Raw stored Google Play purchase tokens and the deleted Homi account association are removed from Homi's historical billing records.

Device-local data

Some Homi information can exist only on a specific phone. If Concept Lab processes an external account deletion request, data that exists only on a phone cannot be remotely erased from that phone.

If you still have the device, you can erase local Homi household data from Homi & account → Erase data from this phone, clear Homi's Android app data, or uninstall Homi.

Google Play subscription

Deleting Homi does not cancel a Google Play Homi+ subscription.

If you also want to stop Homi+ billing, cancel or manage the subscription separately through Google Play. Account deletion and Google Play subscription management are separate actions.

Processing

Concept Lab will process a valid external deletion request as soon as reasonably possible after any necessary ownership verification. If information must be retained for a legal, security or transaction-integrity reason, Homi will limit that retained information to what is necessary.

Privacy information

For more detail about the information Homi handles and how deletion affects it, read the Homi Privacy Policy.
