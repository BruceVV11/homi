# Homi 0.10.0+14 — Commercial guardrails and emergency-region foundation

Date: 2026-09-11
Status: source candidate; Windows Flutter gate, Firestore emulator gate, governed backend deployment and device acceptance pending

## Why this pass exists

0.9.2 established the approved People/Safety experience and Google Places integration. 0.10.0 creates the technical boundaries required before Homi can safely add paid continuous-location plans or expand emergency shortcuts beyond South Africa.

This pass deliberately does **not** pretend Google Play billing is active. No client-only purchase callback grants entitlement.

## Homi+ commercial contract

The source now records the approved first pricing/seat model:

- Free: R0, no paid continuous sender seat once enforcement is activated;
- Homi+ Personal: R19.99/month, one sender seat;
- Homi+ Duo: R34.99/month, two sender seats under one payer;
- Homi+ Household: R49.99/month or R499.99/year, up to four shared-Household members plus future/full Household cloud synchronization;
- every paid sender seat has up to five active trusted live viewers;
- receiving another person's shared location remains free;
- Duo members do not need to live in the same home;
- privacy, revoke, stop-sharing, check-in disable and deletion are never paywalled.

The plan model intentionally contains no Google Play product IDs until the Play catalog exists.

## Continuous-location cost protection

- client latest-location cloud-write guard raised from 30 seconds to 90 seconds;
- Firestore independently rejects latest-location repeat writes inside 90 seconds;
- `setLocationShare` keeps its deployed callable name but gains a hard maximum of five active outbound live-location viewers per sender;
- disabling/revoking a viewer remains available even after a connection disappears and is not blocked by the activation rate limiter;
- existing 30 trusted connections remain possible — only simultaneously active outgoing Live viewers are capped;
- latest-state/no-route-history architecture is unchanged.

Paid entitlement enforcement is intentionally deferred until Google Play server verification exists, so this beta correction does not strand current testers behind a fake local paywall.

## Emergency regions

- emergency numbers are now modeled as an offline, source-controlled catalog;
- leading-zero numbers are represented as strings;
- no global South Africa fallback exists;
- device locale can suggest a supported region without requesting location permission;
- onboarding adds an Emergency region confirmation step;
- the setting persists locally and drives both Safety and full-map emergency controls;
- countries without one safe universal number use service-specific numbers rather than an invented SOS target;
- Home/Work Google Places autocomplete uses the selected region rather than the previous hardcoded South Africa restriction.

Public distribution in each country remains gated on release-candidate review against ITU-T E.129 and/or the relevant national authority.

## Existing 0.9.2 contracts preserved

- People remains map-first;
- exact Home/Work visibility remains separately opt-in;
- arrival notifications remain address/coordinate-free;
- check-in-only background sampling still does not refresh cloud latest location unless Live updates are independently enabled;
- emergency actions still use external phone-app handoff only;
- Google Places setup remains private-key driven and separate from Maps rendering;
- local-first household data remains intact.

## Required proof before deployment

1. Windows `flutter pub get` / `flutter analyze` / `flutter test` on Bruce's actual toolchain.
2. Firestore emulator security suite with the 90-second boundary.
3. Governed backend deployment because this pass changes Firestore rules and the `setLocationShare` export.
4. S25 Ultra regression of People/Maps/Places/check-ins.
5. Verify five active viewers succeed and a sixth is rejected with product wording; deactivation always works.
6. Verify emergency-region selection and representative dial targets without completing test emergency calls.

Do not mark 0.10.0 deployed/device-accepted until those gates are proven.
