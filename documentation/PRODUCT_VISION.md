# Homi Product Vision

## Positioning

Homi is not a generic home-management app. It is a household operating system that helps people remember, coordinate, and care for the small things that keep everyday life running.

The product should feel calm, useful, and largely invisible until something needs attention.

## Core principle

**Your home tells you what needs attention.**

The main experience should surface only what matters now or soon, rather than forcing users to browse modules and maintain large amounts of ceremonial data.

## Primary product areas

1. **Today** — current and upcoming items that need attention.
2. **Home** — assets, appliances, maintenance, repairs, documents, warranties, and property history.
3. **Routines** — chores, pets, plants, pool/garden routines, and other repeating care tasks.
4. **Supplies** — expiry tracking, low-stock reminders, and lightweight shared household shopping needs.
5. **People** — household members, trusted people, shared access, emergency contacts, and optional live location sharing.

## Key foundations

### Household model
A Homi account can create or join a Home. The data model must allow a person to belong to more than one Home even if the first release only exposes one primary Home.

### Local-first
Core household information remains usable offline. Cloud services are used only where they create clear user value: sync, backup, shared state, notifications, and consensual location sharing.

### User-owned files
Receipts, manuals, incident photos, and meter photos should remain user-owned. Prefer Google Drive storage rather than making Homi the permanent owner of large personal files.

### Low-friction data entry
Homi should ask for the minimum information needed to produce a useful outcome. It should not become a full inventory, calendar, finance, messaging, or smart-home platform unless there is a clear product reason.

## Brand direction

Working/approved name: **Homi**

Approved direction: warm, friendly, rounded, modern, approachable.

Initial palette direction:
- Coral: `#FF6B5E`
- Peach: `#FFB08A`
- Sage: `#A7B89F`
- Cream: `#FFF8F2`
- Slate: `#2E2E2E`

Typography direction: rounded, highly legible sans-serif. Nunito is the current reference direction until final font licensing and in-app testing are completed.

Brand tone: natural, useful, calm, and human. Avoid childish gamification, generic AI motivation, or overly sentimental copy.

## What Homi deliberately does not become

At initial launch Homi should not attempt to be:
- a full calendar replacement
- a full grocery inventory system
- a household chat app
- a banking or budgeting app
- a social network
- a family location product that hides or weakens consent
- a smart-home hub
- an AI chatbot wrapped around otherwise simple tasks

The system may expand later, but every addition must preserve the product promise and avoid turning Homi into a high-maintenance utility.
