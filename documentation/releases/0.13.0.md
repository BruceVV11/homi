## Purpose

0.13 adds the production-oriented Homi+ purchase and entitlement architecture on top of the 0.12 canonical Household/data-plane work. It also carries the two small Household UX refinements from the final 0.12 device review so they are not released as a separate micro-pass.

Nothing in this branch is allowed to grant paid access solely from Flutter purchase state. Google Play is the billing source; the Homi backend is the capability authority.

## Household UX folded into this pass

### Stable Household streams

The Household screen previously recreated Firestore streams whenever `_busy` changed. A mutation such as canceling an invitation therefore caused the `StreamBuilder` to see a new stream and briefly return to a loading state, which looked like a page refresh/glitch.

`HouseholdService` now reuses stable current-Household, member and invitation streams for the current authenticated UID. Account changes clear those cached stream references. This removes the same avoidable loading flash across cancel, rename, invite, remove, ownership transfer and delete actions rather than patching only the visible Cancel button.

### Add-to-Household sheet balance

The candidate selector now ends with a small privacy/context panel:

> Household membership does not turn on location sharing. Each person keeps location sharing as a separate opt-in choice.

This gives the short one-person sheet useful visual weight instead of artificial blank space while reinforcing an existing privacy rule.

## Approved Homi+ contract

- Free — R0.
- Personal — R19.99/month; one continuous-location sender seat; up to five live viewers.
- Duo — R34.99/month; purchaser plus one accepted trusted Homi connection as sender seats; each up to five viewers; seven-day reassignment cooldown.
- Household — R49.99/month or R499.99/year; up to four canonical Household members; each member receives sender entitlement; shared Household cloud functionality is the main paid value.
- Receiving a trusted person's live location remains free.
- Stop sharing, revoke access, leave where allowed, erase local data and delete the Homi account are never paywalled.

## Flutter billing boundary

0.13 adds:

- `HomiPlayBillingCatalog` — one governed source for durable public Play product/base-plan IDs;
- `HomiBillingService` — Play product query, purchase stream, purchase launch and restore handling;
- `HomiEntitlementService` — read-only server-authoritative capability state;
- `HomiPlusManagementService` — Duo seat management;
- `HomiPlusPage` — current plan, Play-localized pricing, purchase confirmation, restore, Google Play management and Duo seat UI;
- Profile Settings → **Homi+ → Plans & billing** entry.

The direct Android billing plugin is intentionally pinned to `in_app_purchase_android 0.5.0`. That release uses Google Play Billing Library 8 while remaining compatible with Homi's current Flutter 3.41.5 / Dart 3.11.3 toolchain. Later 0.5.x plugin releases raise the Dart floor beyond the current Homi toolchain.

The purchase client sends a SHA-256-derived opaque Homi account identifier to Google Play. Raw Homi UID values are not used as the Play obfuscated account ID.

A purchased/restored token is sent to the App-Check-protected Homi backend. Flutter never grants Homi+ directly from a local `PurchaseStatus.purchased` callback.

## Recommended Play catalog shape

Use **one Google Play subscription product** for Homi+, with separate base plans for tiers/cadence. This avoids creating unrelated simultaneously billable subscription products for what is one Homi+ membership family and lets Google Play own same-subscription plan switching.

Proposed durable IDs to create and verify in Play Console:

- subscription product: `homi_plus`
- base plan: `personal-monthly`
- base plan: `duo-monthly`
- base plan: `household-monthly`
- base plan: `household-annual`

The source catalogs remain blank until those exact identifiers actually exist in Play Console. Billing UI therefore fails closed and cannot launch a purchase from an invented product ID.

Do not add annual Personal or Duo base plans until pricing is separately approved.

## Server verification and acknowledgement

0.13 adds `functions/billing.js` and supporting policy/catalog modules.

The backend:

1. requires Firebase Auth plus App Check for purchase verification;
2. rate-limits billing verification and Duo-seat mutations;
3. verifies purchase tokens against Android Publisher `purchases.subscriptionsv2.get` for permanent package `za.co.theconceptlab.homi`;
4. requires the Play `obfuscatedExternalAccountId` to match the signed-in Homi account's expected opaque hash;
5. maps exact product + base-plan IDs to a Homi+ plan;
6. stores raw purchase tokens only in backend-only Firestore state;
7. acknowledges a new subscription through the Android Publisher API only when Google reports acknowledgement pending;
8. writes backend-only coverage records and a narrow self-readable entitlement projection;
9. treats active, grace-period and voluntarily canceled-but-not-expired subscriptions as entitled; hold/paused/expired/pending states do not grant paid capability;
10. prevents an older superseded purchase token from regaining authority after a plan change.

The client does not send a second acknowledgement after the server has acknowledged the purchase.

## Multi-source entitlement safety

A Homi account can theoretically receive coverage from more than one source, for example:

- its own Personal subscription; and
- somebody else's Household subscription.

Therefore one purchase lifecycle must never overwrite or delete another valid entitlement source.

0.13 stores server-only `billingCoverage` records per purchase/recipient and recomputes `entitlements/{uid}` as a projection across all sources. If one source expires while another remains active, paid capability remains available from the surviving source.

## Duo seats

- The purchaser is the first seat.
- The second seat must be an accepted trusted Homi connection.
- Server state, not a client flag, assigns the second seat.
- The seven-day reassignment cooldown is kept even when the second seat is temporarily unassigned or the trusted connection is removed. Unassign/reconnect cannot be used to bypass the cooldown.

## Household coverage

Household Homi+ coverage derives from the purchaser's **current canonical Household**, not an arbitrary client-provided Household ID.

- only canonical member UIDs are projected;
- maximum paid Household coverage is four members;
- membership changes trigger entitlement reconciliation;
- leaving/removing a member removes that coverage source but does not remove another subscription the member may own personally.

## RTDN lifecycle

The source defines Pub/Sub topic contract:

`homi-google-play-rtdn`

`onGooglePlayBillingNotification` treats an RTDN message only as a change signal. It re-fetches authoritative current state from the Android Publisher API before changing entitlement state.

The governed deployment refuses billing deployment until:

- the source-controlled Play catalog IDs are populated;
- Android Publisher API is enabled on `homi-ee80a`;
- the RTDN Pub/Sub topic exists;
- Google Play's notification service account can publish to that topic.

Actual Play Console API access for Homi's canonical runtime identity must still be proven by an Internal Testing purchase; GCP IAM alone is not accepted as proof.

## Firestore boundary

Client access:

- `entitlements/{uid}` — signed-in user may `get` only their own projection; no client list/write/delete;
- `billingPurchases/*` — backend only;
- `billingAccounts/*` — backend only;
- `billingAccountLinks/*` — backend only;
- `billingCoverage/*` — backend only through the blanket deny boundary.

Two new Firestore emulator tests raise the governed security suite from 23 to **25**.

## Account deletion / cancellation separation

Deleting a Homi account and canceling a Google Play subscription are separate actions. Backend cleanup removes Homi billing mappings/coverage and does not attempt to cancel a Google Play subscription on the user's behalf.

The Homi+ UI directs users to Google Play subscription management and explicitly states this separation. Account-deletion wording must carry the same warning before public release.

## Paid enforcement is deliberately not active yet

This source pass builds billing verification and authoritative capability state. It does **not** yet switch existing 0.12 continuous location or shared-Household behavior behind a paywall.

Enforcement must wait until all of the following are proven:

1. 0.12 shared Household data plane is deployed and accepted;
2. final 0.13 Flutter candidate compiles/analyzes/tests on Bruce's real toolchain;
3. exact Play product/base-plan IDs exist and are returned to a Play-installed Internal Testing build;
4. purchase, restore, cancellation, grace, hold, expiry and RTDN reconciliation are proven;
5. server acknowledgement is proven;
6. entitlement survives reinstall/account restore correctly;
7. privacy exits remain usable with no entitlement;
8. Google Play Data Safety/legal/account-deletion/background-location requirements are ready.

Only after that proof should a final launch candidate activate paid enforcement.
