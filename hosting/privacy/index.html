Privacy policy

Your Homi data, explained plainly.

This policy explains how Homi accesses, stores, uses and shares information when you use the Android app. Homi is developed by Concept Lab in South Africa.

Effective: 29 September 2026 Developer: Concept Lab App: Homi

1. What Homi is

Homi is a local-first household app. Many everyday records can stay on your device. Cloud services are used when a feature needs identity, syncing, trusted-person sharing, notifications, maps or Google Play subscription verification.

Homi does not sell your household records or location data.

2. Information that can stay on your device

Depending on how you use Homi, device-local information can include personal Tasks, Routines, Supplies, Home items, maintenance or repair records, utility readings, notification preferences, cached location/check-in state and pending sync information.

Homi treats device-local state as belonging to that device. The Android release is configured to exclude Homi's private app files, databases and preferences from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Signing out does not automatically erase local household records. You can use Homi's Erase data from this phone control to remove local Homi household data from that device.

3. Account and profile information

If you create or sign into a Homi account, Homi can process account information such as your email address, display name, profile photo and authentication-provider information through Firebase Authentication. Homi can also maintain a Homi connection code and related profile identity needed for trusted connections.

Password credentials are handled by the authentication provider. Homi does not store your plain-text password.

4. Household and trusted-person information

When you use sharing features, Homi can store trusted connections, connection status, relationship preferences, Household membership and invitations, shared Household records, shared Tasks and the minimum metadata needed to keep those relationships working.

Some supported Household records can sync between members of the same canonical Household. Joining another Household does not intentionally upload unmatched older device-only records without the app's defined migration conditions.

Information you deliberately share with another Homi user is made available only according to the relevant connection, Household or per-feature sharing controls.

5. Location data

Background location: Homi collects location data to enable Live Location sharing and Home or Work arrival check-ins even when Homi is closed or not in use. Background location is used only while one of those features is turned on.

Live Location is shared only with connected people you explicitly choose. Arrival check-ins are sent only to recipients you choose.

Homi can store your latest shared location and related status needed to show freshness, accuracy or battery state. Homi is designed around the latest useful location state rather than building a default long-term travel history.

If you save Home or Work for arrival check-ins, Homi stores the information needed to detect those arrivals. Exact saved place details are exposed to selected recipients only when you separately enable that sharing option.

Android displays a foreground-service notification while Homi is actively using background location for Live Location or Arrival Check-ins. You can stop those features at any time.

6. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Homi can store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token, device registration information and your notification-category choices so messages can be routed to that installation.

Push delivery depends on Google/Firebase services, network availability and Android device settings. Homi notifications are not a guaranteed emergency-delivery system.

7. Homi+ and Google Play billing

If you use Homi+, Google Play handles payment processing. Homi receives and verifies subscription information needed to determine entitlement, such as the Homi+ product, base plan, purchase status, paid-through time and an opaque account association.

Purchase tokens are handled in restricted backend systems and are not exposed as normal user-readable app data. Homi does not receive or store your card or bank details.

Deleting a Homi account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Google Play subscriptions must be managed separately through Google Play.

8. Service providers

Homi uses third-party services where needed to provide the app:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, cloud data, server functions, App Check and Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Google Maps Platform / Places for map display, place search and related location features.
  • Google Play for Android distribution, Play Integrity and Homi+ subscription processing.

Those providers process technical information required to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy commitments.

9. How Homi uses information

Homi uses information only for purposes connected to the service, including:

  • providing local household organisation features;
  • signing you in and maintaining your Homi identity;
  • syncing authorised Household data;
  • connecting trusted people and applying your sharing choices;
  • providing Live Location and Arrival Check-ins when you enable them;
  • delivering notifications you have allowed;
  • verifying Homi+ subscription status and entitlement;
  • protecting the service against misuse, replay, unauthorised access and abuse.

10. Retention and deletion

Device-local Homi data remains on the device until you erase it, uninstall the app or otherwise remove the app's local storage. Homi's Android configuration prevents that private app state from being copied through normal Android app backup or device transfer.

Cloud-backed information is retained while needed to provide the account, sharing or subscription features you use.

When a Homi account is deleted, Homi deletes or de-identifies account-associated cloud data under its control. Connections, device registrations, current location-sharing state and account identity records are removed. Shared records belonging to other people may remain only where necessary for those users' ongoing Household history, with the deleted user's account reference removed or detached where applicable.

Limited de-identified or security/billing-integrity records can remain where required to prevent replay, preserve transaction integrity or comply with legal obligations. Homi removes raw stored purchase tokens and the deleted Homi account association from its historical billing records.

For the external deletion process, see Delete your Homi account.

11. Your controls

  • Use Homi locally without creating an account where the feature permits it.
  • Choose whether to join or leave a Household, subject to owner-management rules.
  • Choose who can receive your Live Location.
  • Turn Live Location and Arrival Check-ins off.
  • Control whether exact saved Home or Work details are shared.
  • Control notification categories.
  • Disconnect trusted people.
  • Erase local Homi data from the device.
  • Delete your Homi account and associated Homi cloud data.

12. Security

Homi uses Firebase Authentication, App Check, restrictive Firestore rules, server-side authorisation and Google Play verification to protect cloud features. No system can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect access to their Google account, email account and Android device.

13. Children

Homi accounts are intended for adults. Homi is not presented as a child-safety service and should not be used for covert tracking or surveillance.

14. Changes to this policy

If Homi materially changes how privacy-sensitive information is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material product changes may also be communicated in the app where appropriate.

15. Contact

Homi is developed by Concept Lab in South Africa. For privacy questions or requests, use the Concept Lab contact page and identify the request as relating to Homi.
